EXPEDITION SUPPLY SPECIAL RESEARCH


Expedition Supply special research is now open! During the event, you may redeem special tokens [Expedition R&D Ticket] with Target Weapon R&D Tickets or Event Construct R&D Tickets and consume them to draw for various rewards.


Duration: 2026/11/12, 10:00 - 2026/12/16, 23:00 (UTC)


== Reward Preview ==

Extra Reward: A-Rank CUB Choice *1

Core Layer Reward: S-Rank Omniframe Choice Pack *1 [Up until Selena: Pianissimo (included)]

First Layer Reward: 6★ Weapon Resonance Material *1, 6★ Weapon Resonance Material *1, S-Rank CUB Choice *1 [Up until Snow Waltz (included)]

Second Layer Reward: Trade Voucher *2000, Phantom Pain Scar *50, Hypertune Material Pack *3, 6★ Memory Resonance Material Pick *2, Coating Blueprint *20, Cog Pack (XXL) *6


== Event Rules ==

1. Each research consumes Expedition R&D Tickets, the numbers of tickets needed for the first to tenth research are listed as follows:

50/200/400/600/800/1200/1600/2000/2500/3150

You may redeem special tokens [Expedition R&D Ticket] with Target Weapon R&D Tickets or Event Construct R&D Tickets at a ratio of 1:1.

2. All rewards are limited, and cannot be obtained again after drawing.

3. S-Rank Omniframe Choice Pack has a chance to drop starting from the fifth research.

4. Finish 8 researches to obtain the extra reward A-Rank CUB Choice *1. The core layer reward is guaranteed within 10 researches.

5. The Expedition Supply is an independent research pool. The guarantee gauge is not shared with other researches and will not be inherited after the event. When the event ends, the unconsumed Expedition R&D Tickets will be converted to Basic Construct R&D Tickets at a ratio of 1:1.
